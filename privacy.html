Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Renmai ("人脉", "we", "us") is a personal relationship and social assistant at https://renmai.onegent.one/ (also reachable at https://renmai-agent.fly.dev/). It is operated by its independent developer, Ziwei Guo. Contact: gzw13979725269@gmail.com.

1. Where your data lives

Renmai is a hosted service. Your account, the data you connect or import, and the sign-in tokens for the services you connect are stored on our server, hosted by Fly.io in the United States (San Jose), on an encrypted disk. Sign-in tokens are additionally encrypted by Renmai before they are written. Only you can see your data in the app. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it except as described in section 7.

2. Data Renmai accesses, and only when you connect it

  • Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Contacts (Google sign-in): read your mail (gmail.readonly) to remember who you talk to and what you discussed, send a message only when you approve that exact message (gmail.send), and read your calendar and contacts (calendar.readonly, contacts.readonly) to know who you met and how to reach them.
  • iMessage and WhatsApp, only if you install the Renmai connector on your own Mac: it reads your Messages and WhatsApp Desktop history on that Mac and sends it to your Renmai account; it sends an iMessage only after you approve it.
  • LinkedIn, X, contacts files: only the data exports you upload yourself.
  • Publishing (optional): see section 3.

3. Publishing to YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and Threads

Renmai lets you write one post (photos or a video plus text) and publish it to the accounts you choose to connect: YouTube, Instagram (professional accounts), Facebook Pages you manage, and Threads. You connect each account yourself through that platform's official sign-in page and can disconnect it at any time on the Publish page. Nothing is ever published until you tap Publish for that specific post, and only to the platforms you selected for it.

  • YouTube (youtube.upload, youtube.readonly): we upload the video you publish to your own channel, read your channel's name and ID to show where the post goes, and read the upload and processing status of that video to tell you when it is live. We do not read your other videos, playlists, subscriptions, comments or analytics.
  • Instagram (instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_content_publish): we read your professional account's username and ID, publish the photos or video you chose with your text, and read the link to the new post.
  • Facebook (pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts): we read the Pages you manage, create the post you wrote on the Page, and read that post's link and status. We never post to your personal profile.
  • Threads (threads_basic, threads_content_publish): we read your Threads username and ID, publish the post you wrote, and read its link.

We use this data only to publish the posts you ask us to publish, show which account each post goes to, tell you whether each post went live, and show your recent publishing history. Instagram, Facebook and Threads fetch your photos and videos from a web address: for each file you upload we create a long, random, unguessable link on our server that needs no sign-in so those platforms can download it. It stops working after 2 days and is not listed anywhere. YouTube receives your video directly from our server.

4. AI processing

To summarize conversations, draft messages, suggest who to reach out to and suggest post text, Renmai sends the relevant text to OpenAI through its API, only when a feature needs it (for example, drafting a reply sends that conversation). OpenAI returns the result to us. Under OpenAI's API terms, data sent through the API is not used to train OpenAI's models. We never use data received from Google APIs, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook or Threads to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

5. Google API Services and YouTube

Renmai's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Renmai uses YouTube API Services. By using Renmai's YouTube features you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy describes how Google handles your data. You can revoke Renmai's access to your Google and YouTube data at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Your use of Instagram, Facebook and Threads through Renmai is also subject to Meta's Privacy Policy.

6. How long we keep data

  • Your account data and connected data: until you delete it or your account.
  • Photos and videos uploaded for a post: deleted within 2 days after the post has finished, or within 2 days after upload if you never publish them; their public links stop working 2 days after upload.
  • Publishing history (your text, which platforms, the links and IDs each platform returned): deleted 30 days after the post.
  • Sign-in tokens for Google, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and Threads: kept, encrypted, until you disconnect that account or delete your Renmai account. Disconnecting deletes the token at once; for YouTube we also revoke it with Google.
  • Backups: the server keeps one daily copy of your database for 14 days, and the hosting provider keeps disk snapshots for 5 days. Deleted data disappears from them when they expire.

7. Who receives data

  • The platform you publish to or send through (Google/Gmail/YouTube, Meta for Instagram, Facebook and Threads), which receives the message or post you approved.
  • Service providers that run Renmai: Fly.io (hosting and storage), OpenAI (AI features, section 4), and Telegram if you choose to talk to Renmai there.
  • Authorities, when required by law.

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

8. Your choices and deletion

  • Disconnect a publishing account on the Publish page, or a mailbox on the Connect page.
  • Export your data, sign out everywhere, revoke Google access or delete your account in Settings > Account.
  • You can also remove Renmai from a platform's own settings (Google: the link in section 5; Facebook and Instagram: Settings > Apps and websites; Threads: Settings > Account > Website permissions). Renmai then can no longer use that account; to delete what Renmai already stored, disconnect it in the app or email us.
  • Email gzw13979725269@gmail.com from the address you use with Renmai to ask for deletion; we complete it within 7 days. Step-by-step: How to delete your data.

Deleting data in Renmai does not delete messages you sent or posts you published on other platforms; delete those there.

9. Children

Renmai is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes and contact

We will update this page, with a new date, when our practices change. Questions or complaints: gzw13979725269@gmail.com. We reply within 7 days.
