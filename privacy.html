Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026 (chat gateway added)

Personal Social Agent ("the app") is software that you install and run on your own computer. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and where it is stored.

1. Data the app accesses

Only with your permission, through Google's sign-in screen, the app may access:

  • Gmail (read and send): to find the people you correspond with, notice when someone replies, and send an email only after you approve that exact draft.
  • Google Calendar (read only): to prepare you before upcoming meetings.
  • Google Contacts (read only): to add your saved contacts to your contact list.

You may also choose to import data exports from other services (for example LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, your phone's address book or iMessage history). The app reads only what you import.

2. How the data is used

  • To show you your own contacts, conversation history, reminders and suggested next steps.
  • To draft messages for you to review. Nothing is sent without your explicit approval.
  • To answer your questions about your own relationships.

Data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone except as described in section 4.

3. Where the data is stored

All data is stored in a database on your own computer. The developer of the app does not run a server that receives, collects or stores your data, and has no access to it. Sign-in tokens are stored on your computer only.

4. AI processing

To summarize conversations and draft messages, the app sends the relevant text to the Claude AI service (Anthropic) using your own Claude account, from your computer. This happens only to provide features you use. Data obtained through Google APIs is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

5. Chat apps (optional)

If you connect Telegram, WhatsApp, Instagram or Messenger, you chat with the app's official account on that service. Your messages to that account pass through a small gateway run by the developer on Cloudflare, which forwards them to your own computer and sends the app's replies back. The gateway keeps a message only until your computer collects it (at most 7 days if it stays offline) and stores nothing else about the conversation except which chat belongs to which computer. It never receives your Google data. Messages you send on those services are also subject to their own privacy policies.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Your control

  • You can disconnect Google at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You can export or delete any person's data from within the app, or delete the app's data folder entirely.
  • You can pause the app at any time; while paused it sends nothing.

8. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.

9. Changes and contact
