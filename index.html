Renmai · 人脉

A personal relationship assistant. It remembers the people you know across email, iMessage, WhatsApp and LinkedIn, tells you who to reach out to and why now, drafts messages in your voice, and can publish one post to YouTube, Instagram, Facebook Pages and Threads. Nothing is ever sent or posted without your explicit approval.

Renmai is a hosted service, currently by invitation: https://renmai.onegent.one/ (also https://renmai-agent.fly.dev/).
