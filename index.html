Personal Social Agent

A personal relationship assistant that runs on your own computer. It remembers the people you know, reminds you who you have not talked to in a while, and drafts messages in your voice. Nothing is ever sent without your explicit approval.

What it does

  • Builds your contact list from your own mailbox, address book and the data exports you choose to import.
  • Keeps a short relationship card for each person: how you know them and what you last talked about.
  • Tells you each morning who is waiting for a reply and who is worth reaching out to.
  • Drafts emails for you to review; it sends one only after you approve that exact draft.
  • Reads your calendar so it can prepare you before a meeting.

Where your data lives

The app is installed and run by you, on your own computer. Your contacts, messages and notes are stored in a database on that computer. There is no company server that collects your data.
